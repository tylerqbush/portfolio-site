About

I help decide what's worth building, then help build it and keep it on track.

My work runs from concept prototypes to an AI course for the whole company.

01 · Who I am

Find what's true, cut the noise.

I'm Tyler Quackenbush. Ten-plus years moving between UX design, project management, and AI-adoption strategy, for a government contractor and a public university. Most recently, I help design and run the 10-day Pre-Discovery sprints that help win federal contracts, and I manage the training program teaching around 1,500 people to use AI deliberately, starting with AI Foundations. In every role, the job is the same: find what's true, cut the noise, and give the people downstream (leadership, a design team, an end user) something they can act on.

02 · How I work

Agree on the facts, then decide together.

Every project starts with a kickoff, and every involved party is in the room, even the person who shows up once and never comes back. We walk through the requirements as a group until we agree on the facts, then we make decisions from those facts, together. I borrowed the approach from how solutions architects build a solution, and I keep using it because it works: people who helped define the facts buy into the solution.

I use Claude for research synthesis and first-draft speed, on prototypes and on the training curriculum I manage at Agile Defense. I still make the final call on what ships.
