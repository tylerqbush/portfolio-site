About

A translator for hard things, whether that's a product plan, an AI rollout, or a sentence.

I'm Tyler Quackenbush. Eight-plus years moving between UX design, project management, and AI-adoption strategy, for a federal contractor and a public university. Most recently: building the concept prototypes that win federal contracts, and running the training program teaching 1,500 people to use AI on purpose instead of by accident. Different surface, same job: find what's actually true, cut what's just noise, and make sure the people downstream, leadership, a design team, an end user, get something they can act on.

01 · How I Work

I start with what actually needs to be true, not what looks good in a deck. Most stalled projects aren't skill problems. They're decision problems somebody dressed up as a status update because nobody wanted to own the call.

I use Claude for research synthesis and first-draft speed, on prototypes and on the training curriculum I run at Agile Defense. I still make the final call on what ships. That judgment is the job, not a step I hand off.

I'd rather ship one honest sentence than five that sound impressive.
