Beyond the Resume

What the resume leaves out.

I've used three strengths and personality frameworks to understand my own patterns. Take them as seriously as you'd like.

01 · How I'm wired

All five in one domain.

All five of my top CliftonStrengths sit in Gallup's "Strategic Thinking" domain. I can't stop learning (Learner), I gather everything before I start (Input), I sit with a problem before I act (Intellection), I look for the reframe nobody's tried (Ideation), and I pressure-test it until it holds (Analytical).

02 · Two more frameworks

Enneagram and Myers-Briggs.

Enneagram 5 · The Investigator

Type 5s build competence quietly before they speak up, and get uneasy committing to something they haven't verified themselves. That's why I check every first draft, mine or AI's, myself before it ships. I also overthink: sometimes I go down a rabbit hole when I should ask for clarity early, and I'm getting better at catching it.

Myers-Briggs · INTJ

INTJs are strategic, independent, and allergic to plans with no rationale behind them. They want the whole system in view before they touch a single piece of it.

03 · What that adds up to

The why before the what.

Together they describe me: I need the why before the what, I check before I agree, and I do my best work once I've bought in. (Capricorn, if you follow astrology: same profile, different vocabulary.)
