Design System

The tokens and components behind this site.

Every sample on this page renders from css/redesign.css, the live stylesheet. If it looks wrong here, it looks wrong in production. The Brand Identity page covers the thinking.

01 · Color

Three colors, many tints.

Charcoal, ivory and cobalt are the only brand colors. Every other tone is charcoal or ivory at a lower opacity, defined once as a token.

--color-ink

#292929, charcoal

--color-paper

#F7F4ED, warm ivory

--color-accent

#315BDB, cobalt

Color tokens that are tints of charcoal and ivory
TokenValueContrastUsed for
--ink-softCharcoal at 82%7.59:1 on ivoryBody copy
--ink-mutedCharcoal at 72%5.55:1 on ivoryLabels and captions
--ruleCharcoal at 20%Not textHairlines between rows
--rule-strongCharcoal at 90%Not textTable tops, outlines, panels
--paper-softIvory at 78%8.63:1 on charcoalSecondary text on dark bands

Cobalt on ivory is 5.24:1, so cobalt is used for links and emphasis on ivory. Cobalt on charcoal is 2.53:1, so it is never used for text on dark bands.

03 · Spacing

A short scale, in rem.

Spacing is a short scale in rem, so it follows the reader's text size. Content sits in a shell capped at 75rem with a fluid gutter.

Spacing tokens
TokenValue
--s1 to --s70.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4 and 6 rem
--shell75rem, the maximum content width
--gutterclamp(1.25rem, 0.4rem + 3.6vw, 4rem)
--radius4px, used sparingly

Layouts change at three widths, chosen by content: 40rem, 48rem and 64rem. Nothing relies on a fixed device size.

12 · States

Clear without relying on color.

Links are underlined, so they are not identified by color alone. Visited links turn charcoal. Hover thickens the underline, and keyboard focus draws a 3px cobalt outline offset by 3px, or an ivory outline on dark bands.

Interaction states
StateTreatment
LinkCobalt, underlined
Visited linkCharcoal, with a cobalt underline
HoverThicker underline. Buttons and the closing call to action turn cobalt.
Focus visible3px cobalt outline, 3px offset. Ivory on dark bands.
Current pagearia-current="page" with a charcoal underline
Reduced motionTransitions and smooth scrolling are switched off
13 · Accessibility

What is built and measured.

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA. The checks below describe what is built and measured, not a formal audit.

Accessibility measures
AreaWhat is in place
ContrastEvery text and background pairing is listed with its ratio on the Brand Identity page. The lowest used for text is 5.24:1.
StructureA skip link, labeled landmarks, one h1 per page, and headings in order.
KeyboardThe mobile menu reports aria-expanded and closes with Escape. The enlarged image dialog manages focus.
TargetsNavigation, footer links and buttons are at least 44 px tall.
LayoutPages reflow to a single column with no sideways scroll at 320 px.
Not yet doneA full keyboard-only pass and screen reader testing.
