04 · The Approach

Three steps, repeated on every engagement regardless of industry.

01

Audit

Read what's already live and flag what's unclear, redundant, or failing an accessibility check before building anything new.

02

Draft

Build the first real version, a prototype, a plan, a decision, before folding back in the legal, brand, or technical requirements.

03

Ship

Test it with the people who'll actually push back (legal, dev, a real user) and hold the line through revisions.
