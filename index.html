03 · The approach

Three steps, repeated on every engagement.

The same three steps, regardless of industry.

  1. AuditRead what's already live and flag what's unclear, redundant, or failing an accessibility check before building anything new.
  2. DraftBuild the first real version, a prototype, a plan, a decision, before folding back in the legal, brand, or technical requirements.
  3. ShipTest it with the people who'll push back (legal, dev, a real user) and hold the line through revisions.
