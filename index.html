04 · The Approach

Three steps, repeated on every engagement regardless of industry.

01

Audit

Read what's already live and flag what's unclear, redundant, or failing an accessibility check before writing anything new.

02

Draft

Write the plain-language version first. Then fold back in the legal, brand, or technical requirements without losing the clarity.

03

Ship

Test it with the people who'll actually push back (legal, dev, a real user) and hold the line through revisions.
