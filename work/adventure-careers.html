Case study · Brand and web

Making a two-person startup look like a company worth trusting.

I built the brand and the site at once for a mobile-first outdoor-industry job board.

Role
Freelance, website and brand
Client
Adventure Careers
Year
2024
Live site
adventurecareers.fun (opens in a new tab)
Focus
A mobile-first job-search flow for job seekers and employers
01 · The situation

Look like a real company before being one.

Adventure Careers needed to look like a real company before it was one. Job boards for outdoor and experiential-ed work tend to look like a Craigslist post that got ambitious, and the stakeholders needed it to work on a phone, because the people checking listings do it between shifts.

02 · The approach

Build the brand and the site at the same time.

I built the brand and the site at the same time, mobile-first from the start. That meant talking to users on both sides of the platform, job seekers and the employers posting listings, then designing a bold, youthful identity system and an intuitive job-search flow around what I heard, with specs and live support handed off to dev.

  1. Talk to both sidesJob seekers and the employers posting listings
  2. IdentityA bold, youthful identity system
  3. Job-search flowIntuitive, and mobile-first from the start
  4. HandoffSpecs and live support for dev
03 · The outcome

Shipped for both sides of the platform.

  • Mobile-first job-search flow shipped for both sides of the platform
  • Full brand and UI system handed off with dev specs and live support
