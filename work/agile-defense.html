Building the prototype that wins the contract, before a line of code gets written

Situation

Federal agencies don't sign a contract on a pitch deck. Every engagement starts the same way: prove the idea works before anyone commits real budget to it, on a clock measured in days, not quarters. Years later, the company faced a different version of the same problem internally, "make the company AI-native" is a slogan until 1,500 employees have an actual path to act on it.

Approach

Discovery is the company's answer to the first problem: a 10-day sprint, stakeholder interviews, and a modified Scrum process that ends in a working concept prototype instead of a slide deck. I designed four of those prototypes, each one a full concept dashboard built in Figma and Mural for a different federal problem.

Readiness dashboard

Risk assessment, threat readiness, and training readiness views for a federal defense client, built around a real multinational military exercise.

DREAMS

A workforce and HR dashboard for federal law enforcement: hiring flow, role assignment, and leadership metrics in place of siloed legacy systems.

GUARD

A municipal infrastructure dashboard that consolidated siloed data sources into one view for faster crisis and emergency response.

COMPLY

A compliance dashboard giving federal law enforcement leadership a real-time view of assessment activity across field offices and agents.

Leadership & AI Strategy

The AI problem needed a different kind of prototype: a curriculum. I'm the project manager and course creator for Agile Defense University, built on the company's own AI Learning → AI Leaning → AI Leading adoption framework, co-authored by our Chief Experience Officer and President. I also manage Experience Studio, the four-person design team behind this work, co-led the Labs project management office for six months during a post-acquisition reorg, and built Fast Play, a template-and-AI-agent system that grew out of our internal methods wiki so any employee can run a workshop without needing deep facilitation experience.

Outcome

Four concept prototypes gave federal clients something to react to before signing anything, real screens, real data, real decisions about what leadership actually needs to see first. ADU is now teaching 1,500 employees the same discovery-first thinking, at company scale.

  • 4 federal concept dashboards designed and shipped through Discovery
  • 1,500+ employees reached through Agile Defense University
  • 4-person design team managed through Experience Studio
  • Fast Play saves hours per workshop by replacing ad hoc facilitation with a template any employee can run
