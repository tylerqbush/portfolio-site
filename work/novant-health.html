Healthcare

Prototyping about 50 page layouts a week for a healthcare website redesign

UX Content Designer · Novant Health (via Accrue Partners) · 2022, part-time 6-month contract

0

Page prototypes a week, on average

10 min

Average build time per page

Situation

Novant Health was redesigning its website and migrating it to a new CMS. Years of patient-facing pages, built by different teams at different times, had to move into new templates and a new design system, and the design team needed layouts at a pace the migration could keep up with.

Approach

I took this on as a part-time, 6-month contract alongside my work at Agile Defense. I worked with the design team in Figma, prototyping page layouts one page at a time, and deciding for each page what a patient needed first and what could move lower. I also helped create a drag-and-drop design system, so pages got assembled from ready-made pieces.

Outcome

Around 50 page prototypes a week, at an average of about 10 minutes a page.

  • An average of 50 page prototypes built a week in Figma
  • About 10 minutes to build a page
  • Helped create a drag-and-drop design system that replaced manual page builds
