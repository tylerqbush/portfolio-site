Case study · Healthcare

Prototyping about 50 page layouts a week.

I designed page layouts in Figma for a healthcare website redesign and CMS migration, on a part-time contract alongside Agile Defense.

Role
UX Content Designer
Client
Novant Health, via Accrue Partners
Dates
2022, part-time 6-month contract
Tools
Figma, with auto layout

Results

~50

Page prototypes a week, on average

~10 min

Average build time per page

01 · The situation

Years of pages had to move into a new system.

Novant Health was redesigning its website and migrating it to a new CMS. Years of patient-facing pages, built by different teams at different times, had to move into new templates and a new design system.

02 · The approach

Decide what a patient needs first.

I took this on as a part-time, 6-month contract alongside my work at Agile Defense. I worked with the design team in Figma, using content and components from the marketing and UI teams to design a layout for each page. Those layouts went to the migration team and into the CMS. For each page I decided what a patient needed first and what could move lower. I also helped create a drag-and-drop design system, so pages got assembled from ready-made pieces.

03 · The outcome

Pages built from ready-made pieces.

I averaged around 50 page prototypes a week, at about 10 minutes a page, using Figma auto layout.

  • An average of 50 page prototypes built a week in Figma
  • About 10 minutes to build a page
  • Helped create a drag-and-drop design system that replaced manual page builds
